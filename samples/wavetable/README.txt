PokeSequencer wavetable samples

Piano.wav is an original single-cycle waveform created for PokeSequencer and
dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.

Pad.wav is a resampled, crossfaded loop from Trevor Exter's bowed C4 cello
recording, CC0 1.0: https://github.com/cleary/samples-cello-bowed
The pad loops while a note is held and has a slower attack and long release.

Harp.wav is a folk-harp recording from the Versilian Community Sample Library
(VCSL), CC0 1.0: https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL

Pluck.wav is a nylon-guitar recording from Meta Open Sound, CC0 1.0:
https://github.com/facebookincubator/Meta-open-sound

Add mono or stereo WAV files up to 10 seconds long to make them selectable on
the Wavetable track. Samples are transposed from C4 (MIDI note 60), so recordings
tuned to C4 give the most predictable pitch. The synth reads files without
modifying or replacing them. Projects and wavetable patches save the selected
filename and synth parameters, not sample audio.
